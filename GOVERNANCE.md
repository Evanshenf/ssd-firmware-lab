# Governance

The project currently uses a maintainer-led model. The maintainer accepts responsibility for release scope, security boundaries, source provenance and final technical decisions.

Architecture decisions affecting a public ABI, persistent format, trust claim or data-destruction boundary require an ADR. Experimental adapters may fail or be redesigned without blocking the v0.x software baseline, but Host self-use, sequential Host/Guest ownership and a real endpoint remain project-level targets.

No AI system can approve or merge its own change. Human review is required for every release, generated artifact and change of license/provenance status.

Governance will be revisited when there are at least three sustained human contributors.
